# Czech glossary

Terms a subagent reading Prague education stats needs to know. Append as new abbreviations show up in real documents.

## Institutions

| Czech | Full form | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| MŠ | mateřská škola | kindergarten | Ages ~3–6. Last year (předškolní rok) is compulsory. |
| ZŠ | základní škola | elementary school | 9 years, ages ~6–15. Entry typically at age 6. |
| SŠ | střední škola | secondary school | Out of scope for this project. |
| MŠMT | Ministerstvo školství, mládeže a tělovýchovy | Ministry of Education, Youth and Sports | Maintains the school registry. |
| ČSÚ | Český statistický úřad | Czech Statistical Office | Primary demographics source. |
| IPR | Institut plánování a rozvoje hl. m. Prahy | Prague Institute of Planning and Development | Publishes demographic projections. |
| MHMP | Magistrát hl. m. Prahy | Prague City Hall | Coordinates ZŠ across MČs. |
| MČ | městská část | city district | e.g. Praha 9, Praha 7. Each has its own self-government. |

## Legal / process concepts

| Czech | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| spádová oblast / spádovka | catchment area | A child has a legal right to enrol in the ZŠ assigned to their address. |
| obecně závazná vyhláška | generally binding decree | Each MČ issues its own decree mapping streets → ZŠ. Look for OZV on MČ websites. |
| zápis | enrolment | Held annually, typically first half of April. |
| odklad školní docházky | deferred enrolment | Parents can defer entry by one year — affects effective cohort size. |
| kapacita | (registered) capacity | Legal ceiling. Actual enrolment is often lower. Listed in rejstřík škol. |
| ředitel | headmaster | Decides admission within legal constraints. |
| rejstřík škol | school registry | Official MŠMT database of all schools. |

## Statistical terms

| Czech | English | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| narození | births | The cohort-size primitive. |
| stěhování / migrační saldo | migration balance | Major correction factor for Prague cohorts. |
| obvyklý pobyt | usual residence | The basis on which children are counted in district stats. |
| trvalý pobyt | permanent residence | Legal residence; what spádovost is keyed off. May differ from obvyklý. |
| věk | age | Watch the convention: "k 31.12." (as of Dec 31) vs. completed-years. |

|---|---|---|
| Praha 3 | Žižkov, Vinohrady (part), Vysočany (part) | In scope. |
| Praha 7 | Holešovice, Bubeneč (part), Troja (part) | In scope. |
| Praha 8 | Karlín, Libeň, Kobylisy, Bohnice, Čimice, Střížkov (part), Trója (part) | In scope. |
| Praha 9 | Vysočany, Prosek, Libeň (part), Střížkov (part) | In scope. |
| Praha 10 | Vršovice, Strašnice, Vinohrady (part), Malešice, Záběhlice (part) | In scope. |
| Praha 14 | Černý Most, Kyje, Hostavice, Hloubětín (part) | In scope. |

The MČ ≠ administrative obvod (Praha 1–10 the obvod) — Praha 14, e.g., is administratively under Praha-východ obvod for some statistical series. Always confirm which definition a dataset uses.
